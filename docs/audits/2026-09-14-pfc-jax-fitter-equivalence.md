# The batched JAX fitter against statsmodels, on real sessions

Generated by `scripts/qa/compare_pfc_fitters.py`. The mPFC unit GLMs were fit one neuron at a time with statsmodels' IRLS; they are now fit for every neuron of a session at once by a batched IRLS in JAX (`analysis.pfc.batched_glm`). The two are the same estimator by construction -- Poisson with a log link, starting mean `(y + mean(y)) / 2`, the IRLS/Newton step, and statsmodels' own stopping rule `|deviance change| < 1e-8` applied per neuron -- so this is a measurement of how closely that construction holds on real designs, not a calibration.

**The statsmodels side is the shipped estimator, bit for bit.** `--fitter statsmodels` leaves `_fit_immobility_models` and `test_theta_modulation` fitting exactly as they did: a `j1620210710_02_r1_mPFC` bundle produced by this tree *before* any of the fitter work and the statsmodels bundle compared here agree at 0.0 on all 69 numeric columns of the statistics table, on every categorical column, and on all five modulation arrays.

**How the runs were made.** Both sides ran three sessions at once on a 96-core host: each statsmodels run single-threaded, each JAX run with its batched fits on its own free A100 (GPUs 3, 4 and 2; the four cards carrying other users' jobs were skipped by `analysis/pfc/gpu.py`) and its statsmodels reference fits on 8 threads. Peak GPU memory was 1.5 GB (j16), 2.5 GB (chimi) and 4.6 GB (wilbur) — `XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false` with a 0.5 memory fraction, so the cards stayed available. The statsmodels set had the host to itself for the second half of its longest run, so the wall clocks are indicative rather than a controlled benchmark.

**Amended 2026-09-15 (not regenerated by the command above).** The GPU these runs used is not reproducible from the lock file: `uv.lock` has no entries for the project's `cuda` extra — it resolves empty — so the CUDA plugin was installed into the project venv by hand with `uv pip install "jax-cuda12-plugin[with-cuda]==0.8.0"` and the lock was deliberately left alone. A clean checkout installs the CPU build of JAX and reproduces these numbers on CPU, more slowly.

**Where the JAX side's time goes** (`j1620210710_02_r1_mPFC`, from its log): 93 s loading the session, 20 s building the designs, **11 s** fitting 74 neurons x 9 models and **5 s** fitting 74 neurons x 2 theta models on the GPU, 617 s fitting the 15 handed-back neurons with statsmodels on 8 threads, and 42 s in the per-neuron loop (the regularized theta fit, which is a different estimator and stays per neuron). The batched fits are 2 % of the run; the handed-back neurons are 87 % of it.

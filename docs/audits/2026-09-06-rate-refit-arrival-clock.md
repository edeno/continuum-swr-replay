# Rate refit comparison

Generated by `scripts/qa/compare_fits.py`, comparing a backed-up rate fit against the current one. Rungs whose **specification** differs between the two sides are not compared term for term; what is compared is the joint reward step (ΔAIC of a reward rung against its reward-free parent, negative = reward block helps), the reward-free `M5_time` goodness-of-fit, the frozen-cohort size, and the per-rung session counts. Where the two sides share a rung and a basis and only the per-bin feature VALUES moved — the outcome-boundary recomputation — the coefficients are paired within session and compared directly. The arrival-clock rungs are fit on a subset of the frozen cohort's rows and are never differenced against a stop-clock rung; they are reported by held-out bits per event and by the shape of the fitted time course.

## What the per-term separation flag changed

Separation is now judged per coefficient, and the structural nuisance terms (`Intercept`, `reward_history_incomplete`) are excluded from the judgment. Both sides below are the SAME specification and the same cohort; only the flag differs. Under the fit-level flag one blown-up nuisance or self-history coefficient voided the whole fit — it voided the cross-validation metric and dropped the session for every term in the aggregation.


**Two mechanisms can exclude a session from a term's aggregate, and they are not interchangeable.** (i) The **per-term separation flag** (`analysis.replay._fit_diagnostics`): the fitters name a coefficient in `separated_terms` when its standard error is non-finite or above `1e3`, or when `SE × the predictor's own SD` reaches `1.0` — the standard error measured on the effect scale, so the threshold is `SE >= 1 / SD` — with `|coef| >= 10` substituted for a column of zero SD, which cannot be judged on that scale. (ii) The **`|coef| x SD >= 10` backstop** in `aggregate_contrast`: a coefficient that ran away without tripping the flag is excluded on its magnitude, read on the same effect scale using the fit's own persisted predictor SD, so a large coefficient on a tiny-SD covariate is kept. The per-term table below reports mechanism (i); the per-emitted-term table at the end of this section reports both, decomposed.

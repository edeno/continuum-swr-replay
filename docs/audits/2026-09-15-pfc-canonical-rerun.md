# The mPFC unit GLM on the shared well-anchored stop cohort

Generated by `scripts/qa/compare_pfc_cohorts.py`. The mPFC GLM used to fit every immobile 2 ms bin in the epoch and to key its design matrices on every speed-eligible non-local event and SWR. It now fits the immobile bins that fall inside a well-anchored stop, and only the events whose onset lies inside one -- the population the hippocampal rate and content ladders are read on, selected through the same `analysis.replay.cohort_selection` functions. The regressor basis still spans +/-250 ms around each kept event; bins outside the cohort are not fit.

2026-09-15. **What this batch is.** All 71 canonical mPFC bundles were refit on 2026-09-15 at git `9879ff0`, under the canonical event definition and with the matched non-event control, on six A100s. It is the like-for-like baseline for the estimator swap and for the control -- not a new scientific result. The estimator semantics are the old ones on purpose: statsmodels maximum likelihood, with non-converged fits shipped and reported like any other, reached either directly or through the batched fitter's hand-back, which reproduces statsmodels to 1e-10 on these bundles. Nothing here treats a fit that ran to maxiter as non-estimable.

**What it does not settle.** 14.8% of the shipped model fits (22,902 of 154,350, over 4,640 of the 17,150 neurons that were fit at all) never converged, and their LR tests and AICs are inside every number below. Figure 4 and claim 20 are therefore *pending*: their numbers are superseded by the fit-validity phase (the held-out/ridge treatment of separating fits), not by this audit. Read what follows as a check that the rerun changed nothing it should not have, plus the first reading of the two matched-control tests.

**The one thing the rerun did change**, and why it is not an estimator difference: 14 neurons in 8 sessions have a complete row in the backup and no fit at all now. They are the whole of the one-sided counts below -- 28 best-AIC labels (14 neurons x 2 label columns), 168 LR cells, 2,814 cells per modulation array. Their run logs show statsmodels itself raising inside the immobility fit (`NaN, inf or invalid value detected in weights`; `The first guess on the deviance function returned a nan`) after the batched fitter handed the neuron back. The cause was isolated on `j1620210706_06_r3` neuron 48 by fitting each design on its own with statsmodels: the seven designs both trees share all fit (deviances 1563.8-1591.7), so does the added `matched_control` design, and the ninth -- `matched_control + non_local_no_SWR`, which exists only on the after side -- is the one that raises. Isolating a per-model failure belongs to the fit-validity phase.

## The population that reaches Figure 4

Loaded with `load_population_data(min_firing_rate=1.0)` from each tree. A neuron enters when its GLM produced a finite primary p-value, its four modulation traces are NaN-free, and its `immobility_avg_firing_rate` clears the gate.

| side | n_neurons_with_fit | n_passing_fit_bin_rate | n_passing_all_immobile_rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before | 17137 | 10898 | 10986 |
| after | 17123 | 10898 | 10986 |

**Decomposed, the population moves in two independent steps.** 10,898 neurons passed the gate before. Holding the gated quantity fixed at the old all-immobile rate, the new tree has 10,986 (+88) -- that step is per-neuron GLM convergence on the same screen, not the cohort. Switching the screen to the fit-bin denominator then takes it to 10,898 (-88): the neurons whose rate over the cohort's immobile bins falls below the threshold their whole-epoch rate cleared. Net +0.

The gated column means different things on the two sides. `immobility_avg_firing_rate` is the mean rate over the bins the bundle was fit on, so before the change it was the rate over every immobile bin and after it is the rate over the cohort's immobile bins. The new bundles also carry `immobility_avg_firing_rate_all_immobile`, the unrestricted rate, and `n_passing_all_immobile_rate` counts the neurons the *old* quantity would have admitted -- so the gate's contribution to any change in n is separable from the cohort's.

## The matched non-event control

71 of 71 bundle(s) carry a `matched_control_modulation` array, every one the same shape as that bundle's `non_local_no_SWR_modulation`.

14,947 control windows were placed against 15,143 non-local events (98.7%); 196 events got none (0 with an onset in no fitted bout, 196 in a bout with no free window of their duration).

The control is matched one per non-local event, SWR-coupled ones included, so it is more numerous than the `non_local_no_SWR` class the engagement test compares against; a source event's duration fixes its control's duration and constrains where the window fits, and nothing else about the event is matched.

Generated by `scripts/pfc/summarize_pfc_marked_family.py` over `/cumulus/edeno/continuum-swr-replay/Processed-Data/pfc_marked_model_family`. Units aggregate to sessions, sessions to animals; `frac_pos` is the median over animals of the within-animal median over sessions of the fraction of units whose held-out deviance improved with the target's kernel, with the range across animals. Units with a non-finite delta or |delta| > 1000 are excluded from that comparison and counted. `peak` is the population mean one-SD slope kernel (mean over session means ± SEM over sessions) at the lag of its largest magnitude; `sign` is how many session means and animal means share that sign at that lag. `pre / post` are the population curve's means over lags <= -100 ms and 0 to +150 ms: a kernel that is as large before onset as after is a stationary rate difference between the contexts the events occur in (the model has one intercept per session and no per-stop baseline terms), not an event-locked response. No p-values: these are descriptive.
